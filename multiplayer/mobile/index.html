Touch controls: put a finger down on empty ground and drag — a joystick appears under your thumb to move. Tap an enemy: lock on and auto-attack it. Tap the ground: basic attack toward that point. Tap a spell (bottom right) to arm it, then tap anywhere to cast it there. Tap the armed spell again to cancel. Pause button at the top. Landscape works best. Pick-ups: green = health, red = damage, grey = armor, yellow = speed.
